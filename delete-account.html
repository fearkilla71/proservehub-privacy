Delete Your ProServe Hub Account

This page explains how users of ProServe Hub, operated by ProServe Technologies, can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your ProServe Hub account, please send an email to:

proservehub.app@gmail.com

Use the subject line: “Delete My ProServe Hub Account”

In your message, please include:

  • The email address associated with your ProServe Hub account
  • Your full name

What Data Is Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, we delete or anonymize:

  • User profile information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account credentials
  • Uploaded photos and job-related content
  • Messages associated with your account

Data Retention

Some data may be retained for a limited period if required by law, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution. Any retained data is securely stored and access is restricted.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 7–30 days.
